# Sample Analysis

Using the synthetic dataset in this repository:

- **9** major incidents are recorded.
- Mean time to acknowledge is **11.2 minutes**.
- Mean time to restore is **122.4 minutes** (about **2.0 hours**).
- Communications met the synthetic target in **77.8%** of incidents.
- **33.3%** of incidents are flagged as repeat incidents.

## What an operational review might ask

- Why did two incidents miss the communication target?
- Are repeat incidents concentrated around particular services or resolver groups?
- Is restoration speed improving or degrading over time?
- Which incidents require problem-management follow-up?
- Are P1 and P2 trends materially different?
- Which suppliers or internal resolver groups need targeted improvement actions?

## Evidence note

These figures are generated from the public synthetic dataset. They are not employer performance figures.
