# Major Incident Lifecycle

```text
Detection
   |
   v
Validate severity and impact
   |
   v
Declare major incident
   |
   +--> establish bridge / coordination
   +--> assign technical workstreams
   +--> establish communication cadence
   |
   v
Diagnose and mitigate
   |
   v
Restore service
   |
   v
Validate stability
   |
   v
Close operational response
   |
   v
Post-incident review / problem actions
```

## Governance checkpoints

- clear incident commander / owner
- documented business impact
- accurate timeline
- decision and action tracking
- stakeholder communication cadence
- explicit restoration criteria
- follow-up actions with owners and dates
